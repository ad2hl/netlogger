# Radiograms Help

## Overview

The Radiograms page shows all radiograms and net reports across all sessions in a single sortable list.

## Viewing a Radiogram

Click any row to open a read-only view of the radiogram or net report. From that view you can:

- **Print** — fill and open the official ARRL radiogram PDF in a new tab.
- **Update sent information** — record how and when the message was delivered.
- **Edit address** — correct the addressee block on user-created radiograms.

## Creating a New Radiogram

Click **New Radiogram** to compose a radiogram outside of an active session. It will be saved to the most recent session.

## Table Columns

The table is sortable by any column header:

- **Date/Time** — when the radiogram was saved
- **Net Name** — the session it belongs to
- **Type** — Radiogram or Net Report
- **Number** — the radiogram or report number
- **Station of Origin** — the originating callsign
- **Addressee** — first line of the address block
- **Sent** — disposition method (e.g., Phone, Email)

## Deleting

Click the trash icon on a row to permanently delete the radiogram or net report.

## Net Reports

Net reports are generated automatically when you end a session. They use the ARRL NTS format with callsign compression (e.g., W1AW, W1XYZ compresses to W1AW XYZ).
