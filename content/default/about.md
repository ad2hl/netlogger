# About AD2HL Net Logger

AD2HL Net Logger is a web-based amateur radio net logging application for net
control operators.

## Features

- Track station check-ins during nets
- Lookup stations on Callook.info or HamDB.org
- Manage traffic and announcements
- Enter and manage radiograms and generate printable copies

Questions? Email contact@ad2hl.net
