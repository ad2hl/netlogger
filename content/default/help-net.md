# Net Logging Help

## Contents

## Overview

The Session page is your main workspace during an active net session. It shows the check-in list on the left and session details, traffic and other net business on the right.

## Checking in stations

To check in a station, enter its callsign in the Station field and press Enter. Yes, it really is that simple.

What happens when you press Enter is the following:

- The callsign is added to the list of checked in stations.
- The callsign is looked up in the list of stations you have checked in during previous nets.
- If the station is found, the licensee's first name, location and a note (if any) are added to the list entry.
- If you have never checked in the station before, the name and location are obtained from Callook.info or HamDB.org.
- The count of checked in stations is incremented.

## What if you didn't hear the callsign clearly?

Put a `?` after all or part of a callsign to search the list of stations you checked in previously for a match. For example, if you heard only the last part of W1AW's call, you could enter `AW?` and if W1AW had checked in previously you would see the callsign in a list of search results. Clicking on the callsign, or cursoring down to it and pressing Enter, moves it to the Station field. You can then press Enter to check in the station.

## Check-In List

- Click a station row to expand it for editing (name, location, note).
- Click the bookmark icon to mark a station as the last acknowledged.
- Click the trash icon to remove a check-in.
- If a station checks in again with new tags, the tags are merged and the station moves to the end of the list.

## Traffic and Announcements

The Checklist panel on the right tracks stations with traffic (`/T`) and announcements (`/A`). Use it to confirm delivery of each piece of traffic.

Press Tab in the check-in field to jump to the traffic entry form for the next unresolved traffic station.

## Radiograms

Click **New Radiogram** in the Radiograms panel to compose an ARRL-format radiogram. Use the Print button to fill and open the official ARRL PDF form in a new tab.

## Ending the Session

Click **End Session** to record the end time. Once ended, click **Show Report** to review and save the net report radiogram.
